# ui-design: components

Part of the `ui-design` skill ([SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)). The radius, focus, icon and decoration rules for all components are in SKILL.md, *Components*.

## Components

| Element | Look |
|---------|------|
| Primary button | `bg-accent text-white hover:bg-accent-strong rounded-lg min-h-11 px-5 font-semibold`, with `border border-transparent` so it keeps its size when the disabled border appears. One per screen or form. |
| Secondary button | `bg-raised text-ink border border-field rounded-lg min-h-11 px-5` |
| Destructive button | `text-danger border border-danger rounded-lg min-h-11 px-5 font-semibold` on `bg-raised`, hover `bg-danger-soft` (`Button variant="danger"`, «Отмени часа»); in a confirm dialog or an inline confirm form `bg-danger text-white`, hover `danger` at 90% (`variant="dangerSolid"`, «Потвърди отмяната»). Disabled like every button. |
| Input, select | `bg-raised border border-field rounded-lg min-h-11 px-3 text-control` (16px, *Type*). A visible `<label>` above it in `font-semibold`; a placeholder is never the label. The field error goes below it in `text-sm text-danger`, and the input gets `aria-invalid` (border `danger`). **Required fields:** on a form that also has optional fields (such as `/register`), the label of every required field ends in ` *` inside `<span aria-hidden="true">` in `text-danger` (6.2 on the sheet), and the input has `aria-required="true"`; a required radio group puts the `*` in its `<legend>`. An optional field has neither and no «(по желание)» in its label. Above the first field such a form has «Полетата със * са задължителни.» in `text-sm text-muted`, its `*` in `text-danger` too. A form whose fields are all required (`/login`) has no `*` and no note. **A field with a length limit** (`maxLength`) shows a counter «11/200» under it, after the field error: `text-sm text-muted tabular-nums`, at the right end, linked to the control with `aria-describedby`. |
| Radio or checkbox group | a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` in `font-semibold`. Each option is a `<label>` row with `min-h-11`; the control is `size-5 accent-accent` with the focus outline. The group's error goes under the fieldset. |
| Text link | `inline-flex items-center min-h-11 text-accent hover:text-accent-strong hover:underline` with the focus outline. The menu links in the header add `font-semibold`. On the night a link is `text-blush hover:text-white` with the white focus outline (*Tokens*). |
| App header | a dark veil, `bg-veil/55`, with `border-b border-header-line/55` and a pink glow under it (three shadows: `header-rim`, `header-glow`, `header-glow-deep`). Its content is the full-width row (`w-full px-6`, `pt-5 pb-2.5`: room for the bottle on the logo), `flex-wrap justify-between gap-3`: the Logo and `<nav aria-label="Основно меню">`; below `sm` the menu takes its own line under the logo and wraps (`flex-wrap`, the same `gap-2.5`), so at 375px a manicurist's two links share one line and the name and «Изход» the next. A guest sees «Вход» (plain, `font-medium`) and «Регистрация», the glass outline button (`border-white/60` 1.5px, `bg-white/8`, `rounded-control`, `font-semibold`); a logged-in user sees their name (`text-moon font-semibold truncate`) and «Изход» (a client sees «Моите часове», a manicurist «Моят график» and «Почивни дни», before the name, plain like «Вход», `font-medium`), the same glass outline button, `disabled` and saying «Излизане…» while logging out; while `/me` loads, «Зареждане…» in `moon`. Every control is at least 44px tall, with the white focus outline. |
| Logo | the n[AI]lbook logotype, a link to `/` named «nAIlbook — начало», bold `font-display`: «n» and «lbook» in white, «AI» on a nail-shaped tag (the `tag-*` gradient, glitter, a glow) with the polish bottle as inline SVG (`cap-*`, `polish-*`, `petal`, `neck`). Its gradient ids come from `useId`, because the page shows the logo twice. 24px in the header, 18px in the footer, at least 44px tall. |
| Footer | an ornament row above it (three stars, a flourish, three stars, `aria-hidden`), then a bar `bg-veil-deep/55 border-t border-white/10 text-moon` holding the full-width row (`w-full px-6 pt-6 pb-5`): the Logo (18px) on the far left and «© 2026 Анина Ангелова. Всички права запазени.» on the far right (`text-label`, `font-medium`, the `copy` shadow). |
| Sheet | the light surface of a working screen on the night: `bg-surface rounded-2xl border border-candy p-3 sm:p-8` with a soft pink glow (`glow-candy`, `glow-pink`), text `ink`, `mt-8 sm:mt-12` under the header. `p-3` on a phone keeps the week table's five day columns at 44px inside the page padding. Inside it the light tokens and components apply as everywhere on the sheets. `narrow` makes it `max-w-xl`. |
| Glass search panel | the `SearchForm` on `/`, the full column wide: `rounded-panel border border-panel-border/60 p-4.5` (18px, radius 18px), a dark glass (`veil` → `panel-mid` → `panel-end`) with pink light from two corners (`glow-pink`, `blossom`) and six shadows (the inner edge `panel-edge`, an inner `glow-pink`, the rim `panel-rim`, `glow-candy`, `glow-pink`, `abyss`). The fields are `flex: 1 1 260px` (side by side while they fit, one per line on a phone), with white `text-label` `font-semibold` labels with the `copy` shadow and white 44px inputs (`min-h-11 rounded-control border-field-soft px-4 text-input-ink`) holding an example placeholder («напр. София», «напр. Гел лак») in `muted`; «Търсене» is `bg-accent`, 44px, `px-8`, `text-base font-semibold`, `rounded-control` (Button `variant="search"`); below `lg` it takes its own line under the fields, the full width (`basis-full`), 12px lower (`mt-3`), as in the design. White focus outlines. |
| Badge | the one all-caps label, on `/` above the h1: `rounded-full border border-white/60 bg-white/10 px-3 py-1.5`, `text-badge` (13px, letter-spacing 0.08em) `font-semibold uppercase`, `text-blush`, with a star-like halo (white, `star-halo`, `lilac-halo`, an inner `flourish-halo`). |
| Carousel | the search results on `/` (`ManicuristCarousel`). A head row: the section title with the counter 6px under it on the left (`role="status"`, `min-h-5 leading-tight`, `text-moon font-medium`, the `copy` shadow), and the counter reads «Показани 1–3 от 6», or «Показана 1 от 6» with one card in view. Then the Swiper (`spaceBetween` 12), with 20px of padding inside it and `-20px` side margins, so the lift and the glow are not cut and the cards line up with the column. **The arrows sit on the outer edges of the first and the last card in view** (the design puts them 12px inside the cards with extra card padding; moved after the user's look, because that padding made «Ноктопластика» wrap and narrowed «Запази час»): «Предишни» and «Следващи» are 44px circles, vertically centred, `-left-6` and `-right-6` (24px outside the card, 20px over its padding, never over its content), in the DOM before the Swiper (so Tab reaches them first): the `pearl` gradient, `border-candy`, a `card-title` chevron (20px, stroke 2.4), the pearl shadows, `opacity-72` at rest; on hover and focus `opacity-100`, `bg-arrow-hover` with a white chevron and a white border, a candy glow, and they grow a little in place (`scale: 1.08`, no shift) when motion is allowed. Every card keeps the same padding and full width. At 375px an arrow reaches the screen edge, so the page box (`Layout`) has `overflow-x-clip`: a growing arrow or a glow never adds a sideways scroll. Then the dots, `aria-hidden`: one per card, 8px, `bg-white/30`, the cards in view `bg-dot`, the first in view 24px wide. |
| Manicurist card | the iridescent search result in the carousel: a 3px transparent border, `rounded-card p-4.5 gap-3` (16px radius, 18px padding), the `iri-*` gradient in its first frame, an inner white light and a pink glow, text `card-ink`. At the top the initials avatar (a 40px circle, the `pearl` gradient, `border-candy`, `text-plum` bold `text-label`, `aria-hidden`) beside the name (h3, see *Type*, `text-card-title` with a white-and-pink text shadow) and the city with a pin icon (`text-card-muted`). Then a divider: a white star with a `candy` stroke between two `candy` lines that fade out (`aria-hidden`). Then at most the first 3 active services (in the API's order) as rows of a `minmax(0, 1fr) auto` grid with `gap-x-3 items-baseline`, as in the design: the name on the left, the price and the duration together on the right of the same line; a long name wraps between its words, and a word too long for the column is hyphenated (`hyphens-auto`, the page is `lang="bg"`), at `text-sm` (14px, the one place where list text is smaller than 16px, so the names fit the 290px card), 8px apart: the name, the `Price` (`font-medium`) and «60 мин» (`text-sm text-card-muted`); with more, one line «и още N услуги» («и още 1 услуга» for one) in `text-sm text-card-muted`. The manicurist's page lists every service. Last «Запази час», at the bottom of the card (the slides are equally tall): `rounded-control border border-candy`, the `pearl` gradient, `text-book-ink font-bold`, at least 44px; on hover and focus it fills with `plum-bright` → `plum`, white text and a `candy` ring. On hover and keyboard focus (`:has(:focus-visible)`, not `:focus-within`: after a mouse click only the active look stays, decided 2026-10-31) the card gets a brighter glow and a `candy` ring, and a 4px lift when motion is allowed. The active card (`activeId`: the first at the start, then the one the user clicked or whose marker was chosen) stays lifted by 4px, and a light (`candy` to white) runs round its border. The name is a stretched button that looks exactly like the heading text (no button fill, border or underline); its keyboard focus shows as the card's `:has(:focus-visible)` look. |
| Studio map | on `/`, the section «Студиа на картата» under the carousel, as in the design: the h2 in the silver style of «Маникюристи» with the line «Вижте къде е всяко студио.» 6px under it (`text-moon font-medium`, the `copy` shadow; the design's «…най-близо до вас» is not used, because the app does not know where the user is), then the map 20px below: 460px high (300px below `sm`), `rounded-map overflow-hidden`, `border border-white/25`, the shadow `0 20px 50px` in `shade` at 40%, `isolation: isolate`. In the top-left corner, «Всички студиа» on top (only on `/`, with two or more markers) and the zoom buttons under it, as frosted glass over the map: `white` at 65% with a 6px backdrop blur, `white` at 92% on hover, a `white/60` bar border, `text-ink`; the zoom buttons 44×44px, «Всички студиа» 44px high with `px-3 text-sm font-semibold`; both get the focus outline. | Map marker | an `L.divIcon` with a 44×44px target and the dot centred in it, as in the design. **Normal:** a 22px circle, `bg-accent`, a 4px white border, the shadow `0 4px 10px` in `accent-strong` at 30%. **Highlighted** (hover or focus on its card, hover or focus on the marker): 26px, plus a 3px `candy` ring. **Active:** 32px, `bg-plum`, the white four-point star of the card divider (12px) in the middle, a 3px `candy` ring and a soft `glow-candy` halo, on top of the others (`zIndexOffset`). | Week table | a pink board like a school timetable: a box `bg-accent-soft rounded-xl p-2 sm:p-4` with no border (the colour sets it apart), holding the week navigation and a real `<table>`. **The week navigation** is its own white bar, `bg-raised rounded-lg`, followed by one `text-muted` line (`min-h-6`, the `role="status"`): «Зареждане…» while a week loads, otherwise the empty-week message or «Маникюристът не е добавил услуги.», or nothing; then the table, with only the board's `gap-2` between them, so the board stays compact: the title («26 октомври – 1 ноември 2026 г.», `text-lg font-semibold text-ink`) between «Предишна седмица» and «Следваща седмица», quiet icon buttons with inline SVG chevrons, at least 44×44px, no tile of their own, `hover:bg-accent-soft` and the focus outline. **The table** has `border-separate border-spacing-x-0.5 border-spacing-y-1 sm:border-spacing-x-2` (the 2px column gap below `sm` keeps six day columns at 44px at 375px; rows are 4px apart at every width, so the board stays compact) and uses the available width, the day columns stretching evenly (`table-fixed`, a 44px time column), with a visually hidden `<caption>` with the week range. **The day headers** (`<th scope="col">`) are no tiles, so they never look like the times: centred text on the pink board, the short weekday in `font-semibold text-ink` above day.month («пн» / «2.11») in `text-sm text-muted`, with a short `border-b-2 border-accent` underline under each (the label has no side padding below `sm`, and day.month `tracking-tight` there, so «30.10» in Montserrat stays inside its column), and the full date for screen readers. **Every other cell is a white tile** `bg-raised rounded-lg`, at least 44px tall, with pink gaps between them: the times (`<th scope="row">` with HH:mm, `text-sm text-ink tabular-nums`) and the «—» cells (`text-muted`, with the visually hidden text «няма свободен час»); the empty corner has no tile. Every row is one 44px tile high, also a row added for an off-grid time such as 12:10, so a block shows its length by its rows and its times. **A busy interval** is one tile over its rows (`rowSpan`), `rounded-lg bg-line text-ink` (`line` as a fill only here: the word carries the meaning, not the colour), with «Заето» in `text-sm font-semibold` and its times «11:30–12:10» below it in `text-xs tabular-nums`, visible from `sm` up and visually hidden below `sm`, so a screen reader always reads «Заето 11:30–12:10»; no names and no service. A day off is one tall white tile «Почивен ден» over all rows (`text-sm text-muted`, the words run top to bottom below `sm`). Contrast: `ink` on white 15.9, on `accent-soft` 12.9 and on `line` 12.0, `muted` on white 6.0 and on `accent-soft` 4.9, `accent` on white 7.4 and on `accent-soft` 6.0, white on `accent` 7.4. Five working days fit at 375px with 44px buttons. |
